ARCHITECTURAL INTEGRATION

WHEN STORAGE BECOMES PART OF THE ARCHITECTURE.

Rather than treating storage as something added to a room, consider it as part of the room’s visual structure.

01

SPATIAL PROPORTION

Aligning cabinetry heights, panel splits and reveal lines to the room’s ceiling and window datum lines creates natural balance.

02

ALIGNMENT

Flush jambs, shadow reveal profiles and precision edge-to-edge tolerances ensure cabinetry feels permanently integrated.

03

MATERIAL CONTINUITY

Matching grain directions, tactile timber veneers and low-sheen matte surfaces tie the cabinetry directly to the home’s palette.

04

VISUAL CALM

Concealed structural hardware and recessed warm LED lighting profiles remove visual clutter, letting the room breathe.
