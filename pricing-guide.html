PRICING GUIDE

UNDERSTANDING THE VALUE OF CUSTOM STORAGE.

Every LumeWood project is shaped around the room, the way it is used and the level of detail you want. This guide explains the elements that influence the final design and project cost.

COST INFLUENCING ELEMENTS

WHAT SHAPES THE PRICE?

The final cost of a custom storage project is influenced by more than the visible cabinet fronts. Design complexity, materials, internal organization and room requirements all contribute to the finished solution.

01

ROOM SIZE & STORAGE REQUIREMENT

The overall dimensions, linear meterage, ceiling heights, and physical storage volume dictate primary framework scale, internal bays, and raw materials.

02

LAYOUT & DESIGN COMPLEXITY

Architectural room nuances such as chimney breasts, sloped loft ceilings, corner returns, recessed alcoves, and shadow reveals require custom engineering.

03

MATERIAL & FINISH

Selected substrate cores and surface treatments—such as grain-matched veneers, anti-fingerprint mattes, fluted glass, and custom lacquers—define the scope.

04

INTERNAL ORGANIZATION

Internal drawer quantities, solid shelving, pull-out trouser carousels, angled shoe racks, velvet compartments, and integrated LED lighting channels.

05

HARDWARE & DETAILS

Concealed Blum soft-close hinges, heavy-duty under-mount runners, solid CNC brass edge pulls, motorized systems, and bronze profiles ensure durability.

06

INSTALLATION REQUIREMENTS

Existing wall and floor level variations, architectural scribing against crown molding or baseboards, access logistics, and lighting wiring on-site.

DESIGN REFERENCE POINTS

CHOOSE THE LEVEL OF DETAIL.

Two wardrobes can have a similar external appearance while having very different internal specifications. The details inside the room can significantly change the design scope.

Level 01

ESSENTIAL

Clean external design, practical shelving, full-height hanging storage, straightforward organization, and refined minimal detailing.

Specification Focus

  • Full-height flush-fitting external door fronts
  • Solid brushed aluminium hanging rails
  • Adjustable structural internal shelving bays
  • Concealed soft-close European hinges
  • Clean architectural perimeter reveal gaps
Discuss Essential Scope
Level 02 • Most Requested

CONSIDERED

Detailed internal planning, additional drawers, upgraded storage, refined material pairings, and integrated room-specific detailing.

Specification Focus

  • Integrated soft-close dovetailed drawer banks
  • Grain-matched wood veneer or dual-tone mattes
  • Concealed 2700K warm LED vertical profile lighting
  • Dedicated shoe display shelves & trouser racks
  • Custom CNC-milled brass or anodized handles
Discuss Considered Scope
Level 03

FULLY TAILORED

Highly customized storage, complex room joinery, specialized internal zones, bespoke fittings, and complete material coordination.

Specification Focus

  • Custom architectural alcove & corner joinery
  • Smoked bronze glass vitrines & backlit niches
  • Suede & velvet-lined watch and jewelry trays
  • Motorized pocket doors & smart illumination
  • Full room millwork and vanity coordination
Discuss Tailored Scope

Important Note: These descriptions are design reference points, not fixed packages or standard price tiers. Your final specification can combine elements from each approach based on your room layout, aesthetic preferences, and budget goals.

TACTILE & ARCHITECTURAL SURFACES

MATERIALS CHANGE THE CHARACTER OF A ROOM.

Surface, texture, colour and hardware choices influence both the visual character and the specification of your storage.

Primary Surface

NATURAL TIMBER VENEERS

Natural wood tones introduce warmth, tactile presence, and enduring organic beauty. Grain-matched European oak and American walnut veneers require precision workshop curation and hand-finishing with matte protective sealers.

Specification Impact: Grain sequencing, manual leaf selection, edge mitring.

MATTE FINISH

Quiet contemporary surfaces with a velvety architectural feel and zero specular glare in ambient light.

ARCHITECTURAL GLASS

Smoked and fluted glass panels introduce visual lightness and depth when paired with internal illumination.

CUSTOM HARDWARE

Solid CNC-milled brass and concealed Blum runners elevate both daily touchpoints and longevity.

INTERIOR ORGANIZATION

Velvet jewelry trays, watch winders, and dovetailed drawers tailor how the wardrobe functions from within.

TRANSPARENT PROJECT EVOLUTION

FROM YOUR REQUIREMENTS TO A CLEARER SCOPE.

A structured design progression ensuring every dimension, material choice, and internal detail is thoughtfully specified before final budgeting.

01

INITIAL SCOPE

Start with the room, architectural dimensions, storage requirements, wardrobe depth, and the way the space is used daily.

02

CONFIGURATION

Identify the wardrobe or cabinetry type, preferred surface materials, timber tones, matte colors, and internal organization zones.

03

MATERIAL PALETTE

Develop the design details, proportions, lighting channels, drawer allocations, and specialized hardware coordination.

04

SPECIFICATION

Bring the selected design elements together into a transparent project specification document with CAD elevations.

05

CONSULTATION

Use the project enquiry to discuss the design direction, material samples, installation timeline, and requirements.
