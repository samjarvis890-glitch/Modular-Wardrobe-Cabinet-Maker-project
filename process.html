PROJECT JOURNEY • PRECISION METHODOLOGY

FROM SPATIAL BRIEF TO FLAWLESS INTEGRATION.

A disciplined, collaborative 6-stage engineering and joinery workflow designed to give homeowners and architects total clarity on timelines, detailing, and installation tolerances.

COMMISSION A PROJECT VIEW 6 STAGES
THE COMMISSIONING WORKFLOW

A CLEAR, STRUCTURED PATHWAY.

Every millimeter is measured, every joint pre-assembled, and every finish inspected before entering your residence.

STAGE 01
Week 1 • Discovery

SPATIAL CONSULTATION

We review floor plans, ceiling datums, lighting angles and your daily clothing storage routines to formulate an architectural design brief.

Key Deliverable

Design Brief & Initial Dimensional Feasibility Report.

STAGE 02
Weeks 2–3 • Visualisation

3D CAD & MATERIAL BOARD

Full 3D digital elevations, internal drawer configurations, LED channel routing and physical material swatch approvals.

Key Deliverable

Photorealistic 3D Renders & Fixed-Price Specification Contract.

STAGE 03
Weeks 4–6 • Joinery

PRECISION CNC MILLWORK

Components are machined to 0.2mm tolerances with computerized beam saws, followed by artisan hand-edging and veneer sequence matching.

Key Deliverable

Precision Machined Carcasses & Hand-Sealed Veneers.

STAGE 04
Week 7 • Quality Control

DRY PRE-ASSEMBLY AUDIT

Every unit is fully erected in our studio prior to dispatch to calibrate soft-close runner dampening, door gaps, and concealed LED circuits.

Key Deliverable

24-Point Factory QA Inspection Certificate.

STAGE 05
Week 8 • On-Site Fit

WHITE-GLOVE INSTALLATION

Our in-house master fitters install the joinery with floor protection, zero dust residue, laser-aligned shadow gaps, and electrical hookups.

Key Deliverable

Seamless Flush In-Wall Architectural Integration.

STAGE 06
Completion • Handover

HANDOVER & 10-YR WARRANTY

A walkthrough tutorial on lighting scenes, drawer adjustment, care guidelines, and issuance of your serial-registered 10-Year Built Warranty.

Key Deliverable

Client Welcome Kit & 10-Year Guarantee Certificate.
