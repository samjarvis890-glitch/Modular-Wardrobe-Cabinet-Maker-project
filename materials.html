PRECISION SPECIFICATIONS

THE ARCHITECTURAL PALETTE.

Engineered surfaces selected for organic depth, dimensional stability, and resistance to everyday domestic wear.

Timber • Category 01

EUROPEAN TIMBER VENEERS

Sustainably harvested Crown Cut Oak, Smoked Walnut, and Quartered Ash. Sealed with ultra-low sheen matte polyurethane to preserve tactile wood pore texture.

  • FSC-Certified Grade A Architectural Veneer
  • Moisture & warp resistant multi-ply core
  • Available in Natural, Smoked & Charcoal tones
Surfaces • Category 02

ARCHITECTURAL MATTE LACQUERS

Nanotech soft-touch lacquers engineered with thermal healing and anti-fingerprint properties. Creates a serene, zero-glare monolithic architectural surface.

  • Deep matte 3-gloss reflectance level
  • Micro-scratch thermal self-healing technology
  • Custom RAL & NCS color formulation
Transparency • Category 03

FLUTED & SMOKED GLASS

Toughened 6mm safety glass available in reeded fluting, smoked bronze, and graphite grey tints. Framed in ultra-slim CNC anodized aluminum extrusions.

  • EN12150 Tempered safety glass specification
  • Acoustic dampening perimeter silicone gaskets
  • Concealed 180-degree pivot hinge compatibility
Fittings • Category 04

MILLED METALS & HARDWARE

Solid brass and aero-grade aluminum edge pulls, handles, and runners finished in brushed bronze, matte champagne, and gunmetal PVD coatings.

  • Solid CNC-milled brass billet fabrication
  • PVD hard-wearing scratch-resistant coating
  • Blum Austrian soft-close concealed mechanisms
Interiors • Category 05

LUXURY INTERIOR LINERS

Supple Italian saddle leather, microfibre faux suede, and woven linen drawer linings that protect delicate garments, fine watches, and jewelry.

  • Hand-stitched perimeter leather detailing
  • Anti-tarnish velvet compartment surfaces
  • Integrated cable management for watch winders
Standards • Category 06

PROVENANCE & LONGEVITY

100% low-VOC waterborne finishes and CARB-2 compliant core boards engineered for indoor air quality and decades of daily residential use.

  • Zero detectable formaldehyde emissions
  • 100% European FSC & PEFC certified timbers
  • 10-Year comprehensive material warranty
