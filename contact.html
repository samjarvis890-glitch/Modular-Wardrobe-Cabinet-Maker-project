CONSULTATION CHECKLIST

BEFORE YOU START

A few simple details can make the design conversation more useful.

01

ROOM DIMENSIONS

If available, keep approximate room measurements ready.

02

REFERENCE IMAGES

Bring examples of interiors, wardrobes, materials or details you like.

03

STORAGE NEEDS

Think about clothing, accessories, appliances, and storage needs.

04

STYLE PREFERENCES

Consider colours, wood tones, matte finishes, glass and hardware.

05

EVERYDAY ROUTINES

Think about how the room is used and what should be easiest to access.
